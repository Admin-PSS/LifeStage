*Getting Started & Feature Reference*

## 1. Getting Started

### 1.1 Age Requirement

LifeStage is an **18+ platform**. Content shared here may be mature in nature — personal stories, adult topics, and uncensored life moments. Before registering, you will be asked to confirm that you are at least 18 years old and that you accept legal responsibility for the content you post.

> **Important:** By registering, you agree that you are 18 or older. You are personally and legally responsible for anything you share on LifeStage. Prohibited content — including illegal material, content involving minors, and targeted harassment — will result in immediate account removal and may be reported to authorities.

### 1.2 Creating Your Account

**Option A — Register with Email**

1. Go to the LifeStage home page
2. Click the **Register** tab on the sign-in card
3. Read and accept the **18+ confirmation** on the age gate screen
4. Click **I'm 18+ — Continue to Register**
5. Enter your **Display Name**, **Username**, **Email**, and **Password**
6. Click **Join LifeStage**
7. Check your inbox for a confirmation email and click the link

> **Tip:** Choose a username that reflects you — it becomes your @handle and cannot be changed later without admin help.
>
> **Tip:** Passwords must be at least 8 characters. Use a mix of letters, numbers, and symbols for best security.

**Option B — Sign In with a Social Account**

1. Click **Google** or **Microsoft** on the sign-in page
2. Approve the permissions on the provider's page
3. You're automatically logged in — no password needed

> **Note:** When you sign in with a social account, your email is automatically confirmed. A welcome email will be sent to you.

### 1.3 Email Confirmation

After registering with email, you will receive a confirmation link. You can use LifeStage straight away, but a yellow banner will appear at the top of your feed until you confirm.

- Click **Resend link** in the banner if you didn't receive the email
- Check your spam/junk folder if the email doesn't arrive within a few minutes
- The confirmation link expires after 24 hours — request a new one if needed

### 1.4 Signing In

1. Go to the LifeStage home page
2. Enter your **Email** and **Password** on the Sign In tab
3. Click **Sign In to LifeStage**

> **Tip:** Your session is saved — you stay signed in when you close and reopen the browser.

### 1.5 Forgot Password

1. On the sign-in page, click **Forgot password?**
2. Enter your email address and submit
3. Check your inbox for a reset link (valid for 1 hour)
4. Click the link and enter your new password

## 2. Your Feed

The feed is the heart of LifeStage — it shows posts from people you follow, plus your own posts.

### 2.1 Feed Tabs

| Tab | What it shows |
|---|---|
| For You | Posts from people you follow + your own posts, newest first |
| Following | Filtered to only accounts you actively follow |
| Trending | Most-engaged posts across the platform in the last 24 hours |

### 2.2 Post Actions

On each post card you can:

- **Like** — tap the heart to like (tap again to unlike). The count updates instantly.
- **Comment** — tap the speech bubble to open the inline comment box
- **Repost** — share someone's post to your own followers
- **Share** — copy the post link to share outside LifeStage

### 2.3 Hashtags

Hashtags in posts are highlighted in amber. Add hashtags to your own posts by typing `#` followed by a word.

### 2.4 Navigating to a Profile

Click on any **user name or avatar** in the feed to visit that person's profile page. From there you can read their posts and follow them.

## 3. Creating Posts

### 3.1 Writing a Post

1. Click on the **"What's your scene today?"** composer at the top of your feed
2. Type your post — up to 280 characters
3. Add hashtags with `#` (e.g. `#mindfulness #lifestage`)
4. Click **Share** to publish

> **Tip:** The character counter turns red when you're within 40 characters of the limit.

### 3.2 Attaching an Image

1. Click the **🖼️ image icon** in the composer toolbar
2. Select an image file from your device (JPEG, PNG, GIF, or WebP — max 10 MB)
3. A preview appears below the text area
4. Click the ✕ on the preview to remove the image if needed
5. Click **Share** to publish

### 3.3 Attaching Audio

1. Click the **🎵 music note icon** in the composer toolbar
2. Select an audio file (MP3, AAC, OGG, or WAV — max 50 MB)
3. A playback bar appears in the preview
4. Click ✕ to remove the audio if needed
5. Click **Share** to publish — listeners can play the audio directly in their feed

> **Note:** Supported formats: MP3, AAC, OGG, WAV. Maximum file size: 50 MB.

### 3.4 Attaching a Video

1. Click the **🎬 video icon** in the composer toolbar
2. Select a video file (MP4, WebM, or MOV — max 500 MB, max 10 minutes)
3. Your video is checked for duration before uploading — videos over 10 minutes are rejected
4. An upload progress bar appears while the video transfers directly to cloud storage
5. A preview player appears once the upload is complete
6. Click **Share** to publish — viewers can play the video directly in their feed

> **Note:** Supported formats: MP4, WebM, MOV. Maximum file size: 500 MB. Maximum duration: 10 minutes.
>
> **Tip:** Large videos may take a minute or more to upload depending on your connection speed. Keep the browser tab open until the progress bar reaches 100%.

### 3.5 Editing a Post

1. On one of your own posts, click the **✎ pencil icon** in the top-right corner of the post
2. Edit the text and optionally add or remove an image, audio, or video
3. Click **Save** to update the post

### 3.6 Deleting a Post

You can delete your own posts at any time. Deleted posts are removed from all feeds and cannot be recovered.

## 4. Your Profile

### 4.1 Viewing Your Profile

Click **Profile** in the left sidebar navigation. Your profile page shows your avatar, display name, bio, stats, and all your posts.

### 4.2 Editing Your Profile

1. On your profile page, click the **✏️ Edit Profile** button
2. Update any of the following fields:
   - **Display Name** — the name shown on your posts (required)
   - **Bio** — a short description about yourself
   - **Website** — your personal or professional website URL
   - **Location** — your city or country
3. Click **Save Changes** — your profile updates immediately

> **Tip:** Your bio appears on your profile page and is visible to all LifeStage users.

### 4.3 Profile Stats

| Stat | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Posts | Total number of posts you've shared |
| Followers | Number of accounts following you |
| Following | Number of accounts you are following |

### 4.4 Viewing Someone Else's Profile

Click on any username or avatar in the feed, in search results, or in notifications to open their profile. You will see their posts and a **Follow / Unfollow** button instead of the Edit Profile button.

## 5. Following People & Discovery

### 5.1 Following an Account

**From their profile:**
1. Click a user's name or avatar anywhere in the feed or search results
2. On their profile page, click **Follow**
3. Their posts will now appear in your feed
4. Click **Following** (the button changes) to unfollow at any time

**From the search sidebar:**
1. Type a name or @username in the **Search people...** box in the right sidebar
2. Results appear as you type
3. Click **Follow** next to any result to follow them instantly — without leaving your feed

> **Note:** If a user has a private account, your follow request will be pending until they approve it.

### 5.2 Searching for People

Use the **Search people...** box in the right sidebar to find users by display name or username:

1. Start typing — results appear after a short pause
2. Click a user's name/avatar to visit their profile
3. Click **Follow** next to a result to follow them immediately
4. Click ✕ to clear the search and return to the trending view

### 5.3 Trending Topics

The **On the Rise** section in the right sidebar shows the top 5 trending hashtags, with post counts. Use these to discover active conversations on the platform.

## 6. Notifications

### 6.1 Real-time Notifications

LifeStage delivers notifications instantly — no page refresh needed.

| Type | When you receive it |
|---|---|
| Like | Someone liked one of your posts |
| Comment | Someone commented on your post |
| Follow | Someone started following you |
| Repost | Someone reposted your post |
| Mention | Someone mentioned you in a post |

### 6.2 Notification Bell

- The bell icon appears in the top bar next to your name
- A red badge shows the count of unread notifications
- A small dot shows your connection status: **Green** = connected, **Orange** = reconnecting, **Red** = offline
- Click the bell to open the notification dropdown
- Click **Mark all read** to clear the unread badge

### 6.3 Toast Notifications

When a new notification arrives, a toast popup appears at the bottom-right of the screen. It auto-dismisses after 4 seconds. Click it or the ✕ to dismiss early.

## 7. Direct Messages

Click **Messages** in the left sidebar to open the chat panel. You can search for any LifeStage user and start a private conversation.

> **Note:** Messages use Azure Communication Services for secure, real-time delivery.

## 8. Account & Security

### 8.1 Signing Out

Click **Sign Out** in the top banner or in the sidebar user menu to sign out. Your session is cleared immediately.

### 8.2 Changing Your Password

1. Sign out of your account
2. On the sign-in page, click **Forgot password?**
3. Enter your registered email address
4. Check your inbox and follow the reset link

### 8.3 Account Security Tips

- Use a strong, unique password (12+ characters recommended)
- Consider signing in with a social account for passwordless access
- You will be notified by email of important account changes
- Contact support if you notice any suspicious activity

### 8.4 Privacy

- Your email address is never shown publicly
- Location and website are optional — leave blank if you prefer not to share
- You can set your account to private (Edit Profile → toggle Private Account)
- Private accounts require approval before others can follow you

## 10. Tips for a Great LifeStage Experience

| Do | Avoid |
|---|---|
| Use hashtags to reach a wider audience | Posting the same content repeatedly |
| Complete your profile with a bio | Leaving your display name blank |
| Engage — like and comment on others' posts | Ignoring other people's content |
| Use images and video to make posts richer | Videos over 10 minutes or 500 MB |
| Follow people you find interesting via search | Following only to get follows back |
| Keep video files under 500 MB for fast upload | Closing the browser during a video upload |

## 11. Troubleshooting

| Problem | Solution |
|---|---|
| I didn't receive my confirmation email | Check spam folder. Click Resend link in the yellow banner. Wait 5 minutes. |
| My confirmation link expired | Click Resend link in the feed banner to get a new link. |
| I can't log in with my password | Use Forgot password? to reset. Check Caps Lock is off. |
| Notifications show disconnected (red dot) | Refresh the page. Check your internet connection. |
| Image upload failed | Check file is under 10 MB. Use JPEG, PNG, GIF, or WebP format. |
| Audio upload failed | Check file is under 50 MB. Use MP3, AAC, OGG, or WAV format. |
| Video upload failed | Check file is under 500 MB and under 10 minutes. Use MP4, WebM, or MOV format. Keep the browser tab open during upload. |
| My feed is empty | Follow some accounts using the search box in the right sidebar. |
| Profile posts not showing | Sign out and back in. If using social login, your profile loads after the first sign-in. |
| OAuth login didn't work | Try a different browser or clear cookies. Contact support if it persists. |
| Follow button not appearing | You are viewing your own profile — the Follow button only appears on other users' profiles. |
